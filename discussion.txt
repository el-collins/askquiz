For the AI model

For your main requirement — very fast responses — I'd seriously consider Groq rather than hosting a model yourself on Hugging Face.

Groq is specifically optimized for low-latency inference, and its API supports streaming.

For image questions, Groq currently provides multimodal models that can directly process the image, so you may not even need a separate OCR → LLM pipeline. Its current vision documentation lists qwen/qwen3.8-27b for image understanding/OCR.

Important: don't promise <1 second for everything

You can design for a sub-second-feeling experience, but guaranteeing the complete answer in <1 second is difficult because:

Total latency = network + model processing + output generation.

For example, if the answer is only:

B. Encapsulation

that's much easier to return quickly than a 500-word explanation.

So I'd make the product deliberately short-output:

Maximum answer: 30–50 words
Temperature: low
Streaming: ON
No unnecessary explanation

And give the model a system instruction like:

"Answer the user's question directly. Be extremely concise. Give only the answer and, when necessary, one short explanation. Do not provide introductions, conclusions, or unnecessary details."

One thing I'd change from your original idea

I wouldn't use OCR first for every image.

If someone photographs:

Which of the following is the capital of France?
A. London
B. Paris
C. Berlin
D. Madrid

You can send the image directly to a vision model and ask it to answer. That removes one entire network/API step.

For difficult documents, you can have a fallback:

Image
  ↓
Vision model
  ↓
Can I understand the question?
  ├── YES → answer
  └── NO  → OCR → LLM → answer

That should be faster and simpler.

how much is groq?

Yes — and Groq can be very cheap for the kind of app you're describing.

Groq has a Free tier, and its paid Developer tier is pay-as-you-go. You don't get charged immediately when upgrading; usage is billed based on tokens.

For your app, the interesting part is the speed + low token usage. Since you want answers like:

B. Queue

rather than a 500-word explanation, each request could use relatively few output tokens.

Voice is also inexpensive

Groq's current speech-to-text pricing lists:

Whisper Large V3 Turbo: $0.04/hour
Whisper Large V3: $0.111/hour

The Turbo model is specifically positioned for better price/performance and very fast transcription.

So, for example, 1,000 hours of voice input would be about $40 with Whisper Large V3 Turbo, before considering any other AI processing.

For your particular app, I wouldn't automatically choose Groq for everything.
